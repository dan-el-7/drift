Drift motion studies

A clickable prototype of the whole window. Ink is the default look; Monolith and Field are optional themes. Make the phone send something, drop files on the window, go back and forth: transfers keep running in the dock while you do other things.

Simulate

You can also drop real files onto the window. Outgoing sends are accepted by the "phone" after about two seconds. Esc goes back.

Look

Routes

HomeYour visibility, the dock of running transfers, Send files. Activity and Settings sit in the title bar. Drop files anywhere to send.
Send · filesPick what to send (the system picker in the real app). Back or Esc returns home.
Send · deviceNearby devices surface out of the centre. Tap one to send. Back returns to the files.
WaitingThe code to check and "accept on the phone". Hide keeps waiting in the dock; Cancel stops the send.
RequestSomeone wants to send: code, files, Decline or Accept. Back leaves it pending in the dock, so you can answer later.
TransferThe self-eating ring, size and live speed, link in the title bar. Hide (or Back) sends it to the dock and it keeps going.
DoneThe puff. Open or Show in folder; returns home on its own after a few seconds.
ActivityEverything running and finished. Tap a running one to open its ring again.
SettingsName, visibility, save location, look, link details (speed and link type are always shown; this adds the technical details).
